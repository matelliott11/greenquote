# Green Quote Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 8 October 2026**

Green Quote is an iPad app that lets trade businesses write quotes on site.
It is made by Matthew Elliott, a developer based in the United Kingdom
("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where
it is kept, and who can see it.

**In short:** we don't run a server and we don't collect your data. Your
quotes and your customers' details stay on your iPads and in your own
iCloud account. We can't see them.

---

## 1. Who is responsible for the data

- **Your business** decides what customer information to record in Green
  Quote and what to use it for. Under UK GDPR, your business is the
  **controller** of that data and is responsible for how it is used,
  including telling your customers about it.
- **We** provide the app. We don't receive, store or have access to the
  quotes, customer details or company details you enter, so we are not a
  controller or processor of that data.

## 2. Information you enter in the app

Green Quote stores the information you choose to enter, which may include:

- **Customer details:** names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and
  what3words locations
- **Quote details:** work descriptions, prices, dates, appointments and
  notes
- **Site photos, sketches and design mockups**
- **Customer signatures**
- **Your company details:** business name, logo, contact details,
  registration numbers, terms, and skip and material prices

### Where it is stored

- **On your iPad**, in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data
  encryption. We recommend every iPad that uses Green Quote has a passcode.
- **In your iCloud account.** Green Quote syncs your data through Apple's
  iCloud, using the private database of the Apple account signed in on
  each iPad. Only someone signed in to that Apple account can access it.
  Apple stores this data under its own [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **In your iPad backups**, if you back up your iPad to iCloud or a
  computer.

We have no access to any of these.

## 3. AI design mockups (optional)

Green Quote can create an "after" image of a job from a site photo using
OpenAI. This feature is optional and only works if your business enters
its own OpenAI API key.

- When you create a mockup, the chosen photo and your description of the
  work are sent securely (HTTPS) to OpenAI to generate the image.
- This happens under **your business's own account and agreement with
  OpenAI**, and usage is billed to that account. OpenAI's handling of the
  data is covered by its [privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy)
  and API terms.
- Your OpenAI key is kept in the iPad's secure Keychain. It isn't synced
  to iCloud or included in backups, and we never see it.
- Avoid using photos that show people or private information you don't
  need for the job.

## 4. Device permissions

Green Quote asks for permission only when a feature needs it:

- **Camera:** to take site photos for a quote
- **Photos:** to attach existing photos or a company logo
- **Calendar:** to add booked appointments to your calendar, and update
  them if the time changes

You can change these at any time in the iPad's **Settings → Green Quote**.

Quotes you email are sent through the iPad's Mail app, from your own email
account. Quotes you print or save go to the printer or location you
choose.

## 5. Subscriptions and payments

Green Quote subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App
Store. Apple handles payment and billing. We don't receive your card
details, name or Apple ID. We receive only summary sales reports from
Apple, which don't identify you personally. You can manage or cancel your
subscription in your iPad's **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions**.

## 6. What we don't do

- No analytics, advertising or tracking. Green Quote contains no
  third-party analytics or advertising code and doesn't track you across
  apps or websites.
- No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
- No accounts or logins with us.

If you choose to share crash reports and app analytics with app developers
in your iPad's settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. These
contain technical information about the crash only, not your quotes or
customer details.

## 7. Keeping and deleting data

Your data stays until you delete it:

- **Delete a quote** in the app by swiping left on it in the quote list.
  This also removes the customer if they have no other quotes.
- **Delete everything** by deleting the app from every iPad, then removing
  Green Quote's data from iCloud: **Settings → [your name] → iCloud →
  Manage Account Storage** (or Manage Storage) **→ Green Quote → Delete
  Data**.

Appointments added to your calendar, emails you have sent and files you
have saved stay where you put them until you delete them there.

## 8. Your customers' rights

If one of your customers asks to see, correct or delete their information,
your business can do this in the app. If you've received such a request
and need help with how the app works, contact us. We can't access the data
ourselves.

## 9. Children

Green Quote is a business tool and is not intended for children.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for
significant changes, mention it in the App Store release notes.
