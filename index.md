# Green Quote Support

Green Quote is an iPad app for trade businesses to write, sign, send and
book quotes on site.

**Need help?** Email [mjajelliotts@gmail.com](mailto:mjajelliotts@gmail.com)
with a short description of the problem, and a screenshot if you can. We

## Getting started

1. Open Green Quote and tap **Set Up** on the "Set up your company details"
   banner (or the **gear**, top left).
2. Add your logo, company name, contact details and terms. These print on
   every quote and are shared with all your iPads.
3. Set your skip and material prices.
4. Tap **New Quote** (+) to raise your first quote.

## Using more than one iPad

- Sign every iPad into the **same Apple account** with iCloud switched on
  for Green Quote (Settings → [your name] → iCloud). That's how quotes are
  shared between iPads.
- **Give each iPad its own quote numbers** before it raises a quote:
  Settings → Quote Numbering (for example 20000 on the second iPad, 30000
  on the third). This stops two iPads using the same number.
- The office email and OpenAI key are set separately on each iPad.

## Subscriptions

- New subscribers get a **14-day free trial**. You won't be charged if you
  cancel before it ends.
- One subscription covers every iPad signed into the same Apple account.
- **Manage or cancel:** Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Green
  Quote, or in the app under Settings → Subscription → Manage
  Subscription.
- **New iPad or reinstalled the app?** Open the plans (Settings → See
  Plans) and tap
  **Restore Purchases**.
- **Have an offer code?** Open the plans (Settings → See
  Plans) and tap **Redeem Code**.
- If your subscription ends, you can still open, edit, print and email
  your existing quotes. Creating new quotes and importing need a
  subscription.
- Billing and refunds are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to
  [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Common questions

**Quotes aren't showing on my other iPad.**
Check both iPads are signed into the same Apple account with iCloud on for
Green Quote, are online, and have opened the app recently. Syncing can take
a few minutes.

**"Email to Office" is greyed out.**
Enter the office email in Settings → Contact Details on that iPad.

**"Mail Not Set Up".**
Add an email account in the iPad's Mail app, or use **Share PDF** instead.

**Calendar booking asks for Full Access.**
Allow it in the iPad's Settings → Apps → Green Quote → Calendars, so the
app can move a booking later instead of adding a second one.

**AI design mockups don't work.**
Mockups use your own OpenAI account. Check the API key in Settings → AI
Design Mockups, and that your OpenAI account has billing set up.

**"Couldn't Open Your Quotes".**
Don't delete the app, as that would delete the quotes on that iPad. Note
the message on screen and email us.

**How do I delete a quote?**
Swipe left on it in the quote list.
